# Midterm 1 self-grade extra credit (DATA 571, Fall 2026)

Decision, 2026-10-30 (Ezra): a self-grade of Midterm 1 is extra credit worth exactly 1% of the course grade.

## Mechanism

Course 1906010 uses weighted assignment groups:

| Group | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Homework | 25 |
| Homework self-grades | 5 |
| Midterm 1 | 10 |
| Midterm 2 | 30 |
| Midterm 3 | 30 |

These sum to 100.
The extra credit is its own group, "Midterm 1 self-grade (extra credit)" (group id 2680677), with weight 1.
The weights now sum to 101, and Canvas divides by 100 when they exceed 100.
A full-credit score in the new group (1.00) adds 1.00 percentage point to the final grade,
and zero credit changes nothing, since the other groups' weights are unchanged.
The points value (100, like the HW self-grades) does not affect the weight.
